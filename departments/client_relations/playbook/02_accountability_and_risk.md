# Client Relations playbook: accountability and risk

**Misses are reported as plainly as wins.** Whenever the scoreboard appears, it stays in whether we are ahead or behind the S&P 500. Name the positions that are down. Where a call has moved against us, say so in one sentence and add one line on what we learned or what would change the view. Never reword a loss as "consolidating", "pausing" or "a pullback".

**Risk parity.** Every idea gets its risks as much room and as much seriousness as its upside.
- If the upside takes three sentences, the risks do not get one.
- Risks are specific to the company (funding, concentration, a key customer, execution), not generic ("markets can fall").
- Say what would prove us wrong, in observable terms.

**Never imply certainty.** Targets and ratings are the approved model's view at a date, not a forecast we stand behind. Cite them exactly as the model states them, with the date. Describe watchlist names as ideas under watch, never as recommendations.

**Past performance** is stated as what happened, over the stated period, against the S&P 500. Do not extrapolate it.
