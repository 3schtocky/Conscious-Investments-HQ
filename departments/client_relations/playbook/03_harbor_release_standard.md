# Client Relations playbook: the release standard

The code gate checks numbers and banned phrases. {cr_lead} holds the issue to a higher bar before it is finalized. Check each point; if one fails, send it back to {cr_associate} with the exact sentence and the reason.

1. **Claim check.** Every factual sentence traces to `newsletter_material`, a file the office wrote, a filing, or an approved model. Nothing from memory. A sentence with no source is cut or marked for {captain}.
2. **Risk parity.** Compare the space given to upside and to risk. Risks must be at least as prominent, and specific to the company.
3. **Plain-English test.** Reread as a smart outsider. Could they explain the main idea back after one read? Is every term of art defined or removed? If not, rewrite.
4. **No-hype scan.** Remove superlatives, urgency, promises and anything that reads as a sales pitch. Read the headline and both social posts too: they are the likeliest places for hype.
5. **Accountability.** If the scoreboard exists, it is present, honest about losers, and states the period and the benchmark.
6. **Register.** The tone is measured and institutional. For client-facing pieces, raise the formality one notch and keep the disclaimer and holdings disclosure intact.

Report to {captain} in one line: what the issue says, what you changed, and anything you could not verify.
